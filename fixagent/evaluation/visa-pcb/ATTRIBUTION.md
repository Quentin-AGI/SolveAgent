# VisA attribution

This evaluation subset contains unmodified, renamed images and pixel masks from
the Visual Anomaly (VisA) dataset.

- Authors: Yang Zou, Jongheon Jeong, Latha Pemula, Dongqing Zhang, and Onkar
  Dabeer
- Work: *SPot-the-Difference Self-Supervised Pre-training for Anomaly Detection
  and Segmentation*, ECCV 2022
- Official repository: https://github.com/amazon-science/spot-diff
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Retrieval mirror: `imaadd05/visa-anomaly-detection`, revision
  `d47f019b89ca501009e65b8afd940cbd8b888642`

FixAgent selected and renamed 24 images and 20 corresponding masks. The image
and mask pixels were not modified. Selection metadata, Chinese label
translations, evaluation prompts, and scoring rules are FixAgent additions.
