# Separate evaluation-approved knowledge from production-approved knowledge

GitHub documents, maintainer comments and historical Cases may be marked `EVAL_APPROVED` after their source, cutoff, applicable configuration and hash are verified, but they remain internal technical-validation material. Only knowledge reviewed by the SKU manufacturer or a named domain owner may be marked `PRODUCTION_APPROVED` and used for real customer responses. Approval never upgrades automatically between these states, preventing public project advice from silently becoming a commercial support SOP. If applicable approved sources conflict, the Case records `KNOWLEDGE_CONFLICT`; the Agent may collect discriminating evidence but may not publish an operation that depends on the unresolved conflict. It hands off when the conflict cannot be resolved. A conflicting safety restriction applies at the stricter boundary without becoming a diagnostic conclusion.
